# 🛰️ Spacewalk (EVA) Preparation

**Purpose:** Prepare safely before going outside the station.

## 🔧 Day Before
- [ ] Review spacewalk plan and tasks
- [ ] Check spacesuit batteries, oxygen and cooling
- [ ] Prepare tools and tethers

## 🫁 Spacewalk Day
- [ ] Pre-breathe pure oxygen (removes nitrogen from the body)
- [ ] Put on spacesuit with crewmate's help
- [ ] Suit pressure and leak check
- [ ] Enter airlock and depressurize
- [ ] Attach safety tethers BEFORE leaving the airlock
- [ ] Confirm comms with crew inside and Mission Control

## ✅ After
- [ ] Re-enter airlock and repressurize
- [ ] Remove suit and record suit data

> 💡 Fun fact: Pre-breathing oxygen prevents "the bends", the same problem scuba divers can get.
