# 🚀 Pre-Launch Checklist

**Purpose:** Make sure the crew is ready in the final days and hours before launch.

## 🗓️ Final 2 Weeks
- [ ] Begin crew health quarantine
- [ ] Complete final medical exam
- [ ] Review mission timeline and crew roles

## 🧑‍🚀 Launch Day
- [ ] Put on pressure suit
- [ ] Perform suit leak check
- [ ] Walk out to the launch vehicle
- [ ] Board spacecraft and strap into seat
- [ ] Connect suit to spacecraft air and comms
- [ ] Confirm communication with Mission Control
- [ ] Final "GO" poll from all crew

> 💡 Fun fact: Crews quarantine before launch so they don't carry an illness to the station.
