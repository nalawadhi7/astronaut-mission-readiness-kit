# ☀️ ISS Daily Routine

**Purpose:** Keep the crew healthy, rested and on schedule.

## 🌅 Morning
- [ ] Wake up (station runs on GMT time)
- [ ] Hygiene and breakfast
- [ ] Daily planning conference with Mission Control

## 🔬 Workday
- [ ] Complete scheduled science experiments
- [ ] Station maintenance tasks
- [ ] About 2 hours of exercise (treadmill, bike, resistance machine)

## 🌙 Evening
- [ ] Evening conference with Mission Control
- [ ] Free time / call family
- [ ] Secure loose items before sleep
- [ ] About 8.5 hours of scheduled sleep

> 💡 Fun fact: Exercise every day is essential because muscles and bones weaken in microgravity.
