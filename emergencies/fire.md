# 🔥 Emergency: Fire

**Purpose:** Respond quickly to fire or smoke on board.

## 🚨 Immediate Actions
- [ ] Alert the whole crew
- [ ] Put on emergency breathing mask
- [ ] Stop airflow (turn off ventilation fans) to avoid spreading smoke
- [ ] Locate the source of the fire
- [ ] Remove power from the affected equipment
- [ ] Use the fire extinguisher if needed

## 📡 Follow-up
- [ ] Report to Mission Control
- [ ] Check air quality before removing masks

> 💡 Fun fact: In microgravity, flames form a round ball instead of a pointy flame.
