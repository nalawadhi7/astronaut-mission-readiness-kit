# 💨 Emergency: Rapid Depressurization

**Purpose:** Respond to an air leak causing pressure loss.

## 🚨 Immediate Actions
- [ ] Alert the whole crew
- [ ] Gather crew at the docked spacecraft (escape vehicle)
- [ ] Calculate how much time remains before pressure is unsafe
- [ ] Close hatches to isolate the leaking module
- [ ] Monitor pressure readings

## 📡 Follow-up
- [ ] Report to Mission Control
- [ ] If the leak can't be controlled, prepare to undock and return to Earth

> 💡 Fun fact: The docked spacecraft is the crew's "lifeboat" home.
