# ☣️ Emergency: Toxic Atmosphere

**Purpose:** Respond to a dangerous chemical (e.g. ammonia) leaking into the cabin air.

## 🚨 Immediate Actions
- [ ] Alert the whole crew
- [ ] Put on gas masks
- [ ] Move to a safe, clean module
- [ ] Close hatches behind the crew
- [ ] Check air with gas sensors

## 📡 Follow-up
- [ ] Report to Mission Control
- [ ] Clean the air before reopening the affected module

> 💡 Fun fact: The ISS cooling system uses ammonia outside the station, which is why a leak is so dangerous.
